Grout That Won't Come Clean

  • Damp air helps dirt settle into grout lines, and mopping alone leaves it there.
  • Watch for grout that stays dark after it dries.
  • Wipe up spills the same day so they don't soak in.
  • For deep grout cleaning, see floor cleaning.

A Film You Notice in Good Light

  • Cooking grease and dust stick more easily to walls and ceilings in humid air.
  • Watch for gray shadows around vents and light fixtures.
  • Run the kitchen fan when you cook, and keep a restaurant hood fan on through service.
  • See wall cleaning and ceiling cleaning.

Dust That Keeps Coming Back

  • Dust that builds up in the ducts blows back out every time the AC runs.
  • Watch for dust on vent covers a few days after you wipe them.
  • A musty smell when the AC starts can mean moisture. See mold removal.
  • See air duct cleaning.

All About Mandeville

Mandeville, on the Lakefront

Mandeville sits on the north shore of Lake Pontchartrain in St. Tammany Parish, at the north end of the Causeway. Bernard de Marigny laid the town out in 1834.

  • ParishSt. Tammany
  • Population13,192 at the 2020 census
  • Founded1834, by Bernard de Marigny
  • ZIP Codes70448, 70471

Things to Do in Mandeville

  1. 01

    Fontainebleau State Park

    A state park on Lake Pontchartrain, with the brick ruins of Marigny's 1829 sugar mill near the visitor center.

  2. 02

    Tammany Trace

    A paved rail trail on an old railroad line, with a trailhead in Old Mandeville.

  3. 03

    The Lakefront

    A paved path along Lakeshore Drive for walking, running and wide views of the lake.

Landmarks Old Mandeville, the Dew Drop Jazz Hall, Fontainebleau State Park, the Lake Pontchartrain Causeway

Fun Facts About Mandeville

  1. 1Bernard de Marigny laid out Mandeville in 1834, and it became a town in 1840.
  2. 2The Lake Pontchartrain Causeway runs about 24 miles from Metairie to Mandeville. Guinness lists it as the longest continuous bridge over water.
  3. 3The Dew Drop Social and Benevolent Hall was built in 1895 and is on the National Register of Historic Places.
  4. 4Fontainebleau State Park has water on three sides: Lake Pontchartrain, Bayou Cane and Bayou Castine.
