Not all water is equal

Clean, Gray or Black Water

Restoration crews sort water into three kinds, and each one changes what gets saved. Pick yours.

Call (504) 285-5666

Straight From the Supply Line

  • A burst pipe, a cracked ice maker line or a faucet left running.
  • Carpet, pad and drywall can often be dried in place if we start fast.
  • Left sitting a day or two, clean water picks up dirt and moves into the gray group.
  • Our home water restoration page covers the drying.

Used Water From an Appliance

  • Washing machine or dishwasher overflow at home, or a toilet that ran over without waste in it.
  • At a business, it's often a café dish machine, a mop sink or a break room ice maker drain.
  • Hard surfaces get cleaned and disinfected before they dry, and soaked carpet pad usually gets replaced.
  • Rugs that come through can be handled with carpet cleaning.

Sewage and Outside Flood Water

  • A sewer line backup, or water that came in from the yard or the street.
  • Soaked carpet, pad, insulation and the lower part of the drywall come out.
  • Everything that stays gets cleaned and sanitized before drying starts.
  • For businesses, see commercial water damage.

All About Belle Chasse

Belle Chasse, Down LA 23

Belle Chasse is an unincorporated community in Plaquemines Parish, on the west bank of the Mississippi River. LA 23, the Belle Chasse Highway, runs through it on the way south from Gretna.

  • ParishPlaquemines
  • Population10,579 at the 2020 census
  • Navy Air StationHere since 1957
  • ZIP Code70037

Things to Do Around Belle Chasse

  1. 01

    Plaquemines Parish Seafood Festival

    A spring weekend of local seafood and music at the festival grounds on F. Edward Hebert Boulevard.

  2. 02

    Barataria Preserve

    Part of Jean Lafitte National Historical Park and Preserve, up the West Bank near Marrero, with boardwalk trails over swamp and marsh.

  3. 03

    Bayou Barriere Golf Club

    A public course with three nines, set along Highway 23.

Landmarks Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base New Orleans, the Belle Chasse Bridge over the Intracoastal Waterway, the Belle Chasse ferry landing

Local landmark

The Base in Belle Chasse

JRB

Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base New Orleans

The Navy moved its New Orleans air station from the lakefront to Belle Chasse and commissioned the new base on December 13, 1957.

Today it hosts Navy, Marine Corps, Army, Air National Guard and Coast Guard units.

Belle Chasse Fun Facts

  1. 1The Belle Chasse Tunnel under the Intracoastal Waterway opened in the 1950s and closed for good on December 20, 2023, when traffic moved onto a new bridge.
  2. 2A ferry between Belle Chasse and Scarsdale started crossing the Mississippi in 1959.
  3. 3The old lakefront air station site later became the University of New Orleans campus.
  4. 4The air station sits roughly 15 miles south of downtown New Orleans.

Good to ask

What Belle Chasse Owners Ask Us

Do You Drive Down to Belle Chasse?

Yes, for houses and businesses alike. Ring (504) 285-5666 and give us the address and the water source.

How Do I Know Which Kind of Water I Have?

Think about where it came from. A supply line is clean, an appliance drain is gray, and a sewer line or water from outside is black. If you aren't sure, we treat it as the dirtier kind.

Is Water the Only Thing You Handle Down Here?

No. We clean soot and smoke odor after a kitchen fire at home or a grease flare at a restaurant, and we handle mold, sewage and deep cleaning too. Our fire and smoke damage page explains that side of the work.

What If Mold Shows Up Weeks Later?

Call us back. It's a separate job where we contain the area, remove affected materials and clean what can be saved. Businesses can read about commercial mold removal.

Can I Send Pictures Before You Come?

Please do. Attach them to the estimate form so we can plan the equipment. Check your policy or call your agent about coverage. The estimate is free.
