Heavy Rain Finds the Weak Spots

  • Clear the gutters early so rain runs away from the slab.
  • After a hard rain, check under windows and doors for swollen trim.
  • Street flooding can push water in under a garage door.
  • Carpet that stays wet past a day needs more than a shop vac. See home water damage restoration.

Getting Ready and Cleaning Up

  • Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30.
  • Before a storm, walk your house or your storefront with your phone and photograph every room, plus any stock you keep.
  • Afterward, look over your own policy or ring your agent with coverage questions.
  • A torn roof lets rain in for days after the wind dies down. Our water damage page explains the dry-out.

When Pipes Freeze

  • Hard freezes are rare here, so plenty of outdoor pipes go unwrapped.
  • On the coldest nights, let faucets drip and open the cabinet doors under sinks.
  • A split pipe often stays quiet until it thaws, then sprays.
  • If a wall gets soaked, phone us once the plumber is done.

All About Metairie

Metairie, West of New Orleans

Metairie lies along the south shore of Lake Pontchartrain in Jefferson Parish, bordering New Orleans to the east. It was never incorporated, so it has no mayor or city hall of its own.

  • ParishJefferson
  • Population143,507 at the 2020 census
  • StatusUnincorporated community
  • ZIP Codes70001, 70002, 70003, 70005, 70006

Things to Do in Metairie

  1. 01

    Lafreniere Park

    About 155 acres of lawns, lagoons and walking paths, built where a racetrack once stood.

  2. 02

    Bonnabel Boat Launch

    A park at the end of Bonnabel Boulevard with boat ramps, a fishing pier and a dog park.

  3. 03

    Pontchartrain Lakefront Trail

    A paved levee-top route along the lake, with a parking area at Bonnabel.

Landmarks Lafreniere Park, the Causeway's south end, Old Metairie, Bonnabel Boat Launch

Fun Facts About Metairie

  1. 1The name comes from a French word for a small tenant farm that paid its landlord with part of the harvest.
  2. 2Metairie Ridge is a natural levee that Native people used as a road before French settlers came in the 1720s.
  3. 3Lafreniere Park was dedicated in 1982 on the grounds of the old Jefferson Downs racetrack.
  4. 4The Lake Pontchartrain Causeway touches land in Metairie at its south end, where it becomes Causeway Boulevard.

Metairie FAQs

Do You Take Jobs in Metairie?

Yes, all over Metairie, from Old Metairie up to the lakefront. Homes and businesses both.

Can You Help Right After a Hurricane?

Call once the storm has passed and the roads are open. We answer 24/7. Our residential water damage page covers what you can do before we get there.

Can You Clean Up a Metairie Office or Store After a Summer Storm?

Yes. Once the rain moves on, we pull wet ceiling tiles, carpet and drywall, dry the space and clean it so your staff and customers can come back in. The commercial water damage page shows the full process.

Does Summer Humidity Lead to Mold?

It can, once it meets a leak. Our mold removal work means containing the area, removing affected materials and cleaning what can be saved. For an office or shop, see commercial mold removal.

Will My Insurance Pay for Storm Damage?

That depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent. Then send photos through the estimate form. The estimate is free.
