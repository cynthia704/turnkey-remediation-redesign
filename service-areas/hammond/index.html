Renting in a college town

Who Does What When a Rental Gets Hit

With Southeastern's campus in town, plenty of Hammond homes are rentals. Pick your role to see your part of the cleanup.

Call (504) 285-5666

Report It, Then Protect Your Stuff

  • Text or call your landlord the moment you spot water, soot or a backup, and keep a copy of the message.
  • Lift laptops, textbooks and anything plugged in up off the floor.
  • Snap photos of your belongings before you clean anything.
  • Ask your renters insurance agent what your own policy says about your things.

Move Quickly on a Property You Own

  • Get the water stopped and the unit drying so floors and drywall don't keep soaking.
  • Call your own insurance agent about the building itself.
  • Let the tenant know who's coming over and when.
  • Between leases, our cleaning services get a unit ready to show.

Keep Every Unit on Track

  • Send one list with unit numbers and door codes for every unit involved.
  • Check the units below and beside the one that flooded.
  • Keep a dated log of calls, photos and who entered each unit.
  • Leasing offices and hallways fall under commercial cleaning.

All About Hammond

Hammond, Where I-12 Meets I-55

Hammond is a Tangipahoa Parish city named for Peter Hammond, a Swedish immigrant who settled the area. Southeastern Louisiana University has been part of the town since 1925.

  • ParishTangipahoa
  • Population19,584 at the 2020 census
  • UniversitySoutheastern, founded 1925
  • ZIP Codes70401, 70403

Things to Do in Hammond

  1. 01

    Columbia Theatre

    A downtown movie house from 1928, now run by Southeastern, with plays, concerts and films.

  2. 02

    Hammond Historic District

    Older storefronts along East Thomas Street and nearby blocks, on the National Register since 1980.

  3. 03

    Tangipahoa African American Heritage Museum

    Galleries on local Black history and a veterans archive, part of the Louisiana African American Heritage Trail.

Landmarks Southeastern Louisiana University, the 1912 train depot, the Columbia Theatre, East Thomas Street

Fun Facts About Hammond

  1. 1Southeastern opened in 1925 as Hammond Junior College, with classes in two rooms at Hammond High School.
  2. 2In the 1890s, local growers shipped strawberries across the country straight from downtown Hammond.
  3. 3The Illinois Central built Hammond's depot in 1912. Amtrak's City of New Orleans still stops there on its Chicago run.
  4. 4South of town, I-55 rides a twin-span bridge for nearly 23 miles over the Manchac swamp.

Driving in

On the Road to Hammond

  • From New OrleansDrive I-10 west to LaPlace, then switch to I-55 north over the Manchac swamp.
  • From Baton Rouge or the North ShoreRide I-12 until it crosses I-55 in Hammond.
  • By railThe City of New Orleans train stops at the Hammond depot downtown.

Hammond Rental Questions

Who Should Call You, the Tenant or the Landlord?

Either one. On a rental, the owner or manager signs off on the work before we start. Reach us at (504) 285-5666.

Can You Clean a Unit Between Leases?

Yes. Give us the move-out date and the next move-in date. Our carpet cleaning and floor cleaning pages show what a turnover can include.

Does Renters Insurance Pay for This?

That depends on the policy. Tenants should ask their own agent about personal belongings, and owners should check with theirs about the building.

What If Mold Turns Up in an Apartment?

That becomes its own job. We contain the spot, cut out affected materials and clean anything worth saving. Begin with residential mold removal.

Yes, anywhere in Hammond. Send the address through the estimate form, or phone us any hour and describe what you see. The estimate is free.
