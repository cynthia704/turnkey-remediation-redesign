Three usual suspects

Where Moisture Hides in a Gretna Home or Business

Our damp air gives a slow leak plenty of help. These are the three places we open up most.

Call (504) 285-5666

Steam With Nowhere to Go

  • Shower steam soaks into the ceiling and the corners above the tub.
  • Look for paint that bubbles, or caulk that turns dark from the inside.
  • Leave the exhaust fan on for a while after every shower.
  • A floor that feels spongy near the toilet can mean a failed wax ring.

A Drain Line That Clogs

  • The AC pulls water out of the air all summer, and it drains through one small line.
  • When that line clogs, the pan overflows into the closet floor and the wall behind it.
  • At home, check for a wet pan, a stained baseboard or a musty smell when the system kicks on.
  • In a back office or a restaurant, the unit often sits above the ceiling tiles, so a brown tile over a desk or a booth is the first clue.
  • Dust in a damp closet rides the air to every room the unit serves, so see air duct and HVAC cleaning.

A Slow Drip Nobody Hears

  • A loose fitting or a worn supply hose can drip for weeks behind the cleaning supplies.
  • Pressed wood cabinet floors swell and crumble once they stay wet.
  • Empty the cabinet now and then and run a dry hand along the pipes.
  • If the wall behind it is soft, read about water damage restoration.

All About Gretna

Gretna, Across the River

Gretna sits on the west bank of the Mississippi River, across from uptown New Orleans, and serves as the parish seat of Jefferson Parish. It became a single city in 1913, when older riverside settlements were joined under one name.

  • ParishJefferson, the parish seat
  • Population17,814 at the 2020 census
  • Became a City1913
  • ZIP Codes70053, 70056

Things to Do in Gretna

  1. 01

    Gretna Historical Society Museum

    A cluster of 19th century Creole cottages on Lafayette Street, next to the Louisiana State Fire Museum.

  2. 02

    German-American Cultural Center

    A museum on Huey P. Long Avenue about the German families who settled the town.

  3. 03

    Gretna Fest

    A fall music weekend in the downtown streets near the river.

Landmarks Gretna City Hall in the old Jefferson Parish Courthouse, the David Crockett Fire Hall, the Jefferson Memorial Arch, the Mel Ott statue

Mel Ott

Born in Gretna in 1909, he played his whole major league career with the New York Giants, from 1926 to 1947, and hit 511 home runs.

He went into the Baseball Hall of Fame in 1951. A bronze statue of him now stands in his hometown.

Fun Facts About Gretna

  1. 1Today's city grew out of three settlements: McDonoghville from 1815, Mechanikham from 1836 and Gretna from 1838.
  2. 2Gretna has been the seat of Jefferson Parish since 1884.
  3. 3The old Jefferson Parish Courthouse went up in 1907 and now houses Gretna City Hall.
  4. 4The Gretna Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1985.

Questions From Gretna

Do You Come Out to Gretna?

We do. Homes and businesses on the West Bank are part of our area. Call (504) 285-5666 with the street address.

What If I Can't Tell Whether It's Mold?

Send a clear photo and tell us about any leak near it. If it's tied to moisture, we handle it as a separate job: contain the area, remove affected materials and clean what can be saved. Our mold removal page explains each part.

Can the Work at My Gretna Business Fit Around Our Hours?

Tell us when your doors open and close, and we'll talk through how to plan the loud parts, like cutting out wet drywall in a restaurant or a back office, around your customers. Our commercial water damage page shows what a typical job involves.

Does My Homeowners Policy Cover a Slow Leak?

That depends on your policy. Ask your agent how it treats a leak that ran for weeks compared to a pipe that burst. Photos and dates help either way.
