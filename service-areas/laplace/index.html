Hurricane season prep

Getting Your LaPlace Home or Business Storm Ready

The Atlantic season runs June through November. Choose a stage to see what to do and what to record.

Call (504) 285-5666

Make a Record of the Property

  • Walk every room with your phone and film floors, walls, ceilings and closets.
  • Photograph the serial numbers on the TV, fridge, washer and AC unit, or on the registers, computers and coolers at a shop.
  • Call your agent and ask what your policy says about flood and wind.
  • Keep copies of those files and your policy online, away from the house.

Lift, Bag and Unplug

  • Move rugs, boxes and papers up onto beds, counters or high shelves. At a business, lift stock and computer towers off the floor.
  • Seal photos, documents and business records in zip bags or a plastic tub with a lid.
  • Unplug electronics and set the fridge and freezer to their coldest settings.
  • Write our number, (504) 285-5666, on paper too, in case your phone dies.

Document Before You Clean

  • Wait for local officials to give the all clear before you go back in.
  • Photograph water lines on the walls before anything gets moved.
  • Carry wet rugs and cushions outside so the floors underneath can dry.
  • If water came in, read our water damage restoration page and call before tearing anything out.

All About LaPlace

LaPlace, Home of Andouille

LaPlace is an unincorporated community on the east bank of the Mississippi River in St. John the Baptist Parish. It takes its name from Basile Laplace, a planter who let the railroad cross his land in the 1880s.

  • ParishSt. John the Baptist
  • Population28,841 at the 2020 census
  • Known ForAndouille sausage
  • ZIP Code70068

Things to Do in LaPlace

  1. 01

    Andouille Festival

    A three-day festival every October since 1972, built around the smoked pork sausage the area is famous for.

  2. 02

    Zip NOLA

    A zipline course over the swamp on Peavine Road, with suspension bridges and a spiral stair around a cypress tree.

  3. 03

    Manchac Swamp Tours

    Guided boat and kayak trips through cypress swamp, where herons and alligators are common sights.

Landmarks The 1811 Kid Ory Historic House, the Manchac Swamp Bridge, Airline Highway (US 61)

A LaPlace Original

KO

Edward "Kid" Ory

Born on Woodland Plantation in LaPlace on Christmas Day, 1886.

He grew into one of the leading trombonists of early New Orleans jazz, recorded with Louis Armstrong in the 1920s and wrote "Muskrat Ramble."

Fun Facts About LaPlace

  1. 1Before the railroad depot gave the town its name, the area was called Karlstein and later Bonnet Carré.
  2. 2The parish police jury made "LaPlace," with a capital P, the official spelling in 1971.
  3. 3Interstate 55 begins here at I-10 and runs north all the way to Chicago.
  4. 4The Manchac Swamp Bridge carries I-55 for 22.8 miles over the swamp. It opened in 1979.

Getting here

Driving Out to LaPlace

  • From New OrleansHead west on I-10, which skirts the southwest edge of Lake Pontchartrain before the LaPlace exits.
  • From HammondFollow I-55 south over the Manchac Swamp Bridge. It ends at I-10 on the north side of LaPlace.
  • On the surface roadsAirline Highway (US 61) runs straight through town.

Questions From LaPlace

Do You Come Out to LaPlace?

We do, for houses and businesses across St. John the Baptist Parish. Share your street address when you reach us at (504) 285-5666.

What Should I Photograph Before a Storm?

Every room from corner to corner, plus closets, appliances and the outside of the house. Keep the dates on the files so it's clear they came first.

Can You Help When Water Comes Inside During a Hurricane?

Yes. Reach out once officials say it's clear to return. The water damage restoration page explains how the drying works, and commercial water damage covers shops and offices.

Will You Deal With Mold After a Flood?

Yes, as a separate job. First we contain the area. Next, affected materials come out, and we clean whatever is worth saving. Read about mold removal.

How Can a LaPlace Business Get Ready for Hurricane Season?

Film the space while it's dry, back up your records somewhere off site and plan where stock goes if water rises. If a storm still gets in, commercial cleaning and drying can follow. Send the form with a few pictures, or phone day or night. The estimate is free.
