A Toilet Overflowing

  • Do: turn the valve behind the toilet clockwise to shut off its water.
  • Do: keep kids and pets out of the bathroom.
  • Don't: flush again to test whether it cleared.
  • A plumber clears the clog. We clean the floor and disinfect. See sewage backup cleanup.

Water Rising From a Low Drain

  • Do: stop all water use in the house, washer and dishwasher included.
  • Do: lift rugs and boxes clear of the water if you can reach them without touching it.
  • Don't: pour drain cleaner into standing sewage. It can splash back on you.
  • Call a plumber for the line and us for the mess it left.

Sewage at the Cleanout Pipe

  • The cleanout is the capped pipe in the yard that opens onto your sewer line.
  • Do: keep people off the wet ground around it.
  • Don't: pull the cap yourself while the line is full.
  • After a plumber clears the line, we clean and disinfect any room the backup reached. See sanitation services.

All About River Ridge

River Ridge, Upriver From New Orleans

River Ridge is an unincorporated community in Jefferson Parish, bordered by Harahan, Kenner, Metairie and the Mississippi River. Most of its neighborhoods were built in the suburban boom after World War II.

  • ParishJefferson
  • Population13,591 at the 2020 census
  • NamedRiver Ridge since 1974
  • ZIP Code70123

Things to Do in River Ridge

  2. 02

    Little Farms Playground

    A parish park on South Park Street with baseball fields, tennis courts and a tot lot.

  3. 03

    Jefferson Highway

    The main road through the community, with restaurants, coffee shops and local stores.

Where the Levee Broke

SC

Sauvé's Crevasse

On May 3, 1849, the river levee gave way at Pierre Sauvé's plantation, on land that is now River Ridge.

Floodwater spread across much of New Orleans, and the river didn't drop until 48 days later.

Fun Facts About River Ridge

  1. 1Before 1974, most people called this area Little Farms. Little Farms Avenue still runs through it.
  2. 2In 2019, River Ridge's Eastbank Little League beat Curaçao 8 to 0 and became the first Louisiana team to win the Little League World Series.
  3. 3River Ridge has no city government of its own. The census counts it as a census-designated place.
  4. 4French colonists and their descendants farmed sugar here in the late 1700s and early 1800s.

River Ridge Sewage Questions

Do You Clean Up Sewage in River Ridge?

Yes. We handle River Ridge houses and businesses. Call (504) 285-5666 and tell us which drain backed up.

Who Clears the Clogged Line?

A plumber clears the line. We take care of what spilled out: removal, cleaning and disinfecting. See sewage backup cleanup.

Can I Keep Using the Sinks and Shower?

Hold off on all water in the house until the line is clear. Most household drains share one sewer pipe, so a running shower can push more out at the lowest drain.

What Happens to Carpet That Sewage Touched?

Carpet and pad soaked by sewage usually get thrown out. Hard floors can often be cleaned and disinfected. Our sanitation page covers that side of the work.

What Should I Send for an Estimate?

Photos of the spot where it came up and the rooms beside it. Send them with the form, or phone us any hour. The estimate is free.
