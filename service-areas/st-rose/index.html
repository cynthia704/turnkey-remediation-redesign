Three common smells

What Your Nose Is Telling You

Every smell has a few usual suspects. Choose one to see what it tends to mean and where to look first.

Call (504) 285-5666

Moisture Hiding Somewhere

  • This one usually means water is feeding growth out of sight.
  • Check under sinks, behind the washer and around the AC closet.
  • Look low on baseboards and drywall for stains or bubbling paint.
  • Found spots? Our mold removal page explains the next step.

Residue That Holds On

  • Often left from a past fire, a kitchen flare-up or someone who smoked indoors.
  • Sniff curtains, closets and the big return air vent.
  • Rub a wall with a white cloth. A yellow or gray smudge means residue.
  • Our odor removal service goes after the source.

Gas Coming Up the Lines

  • Often a dry trap in a guest bath or a floor drain nobody uses.
  • Run water in each drain for a minute, then check again.
  • Rotten eggs near the stove or water heater could be natural gas. Leave and call the gas company.
  • If water backs up, stop using that drain and see sewage cleanup.

All About St. Rose

St. Rose, a River Road Community

St. Rose is a small community on the east bank of the Mississippi in St. Charles Parish, about two miles from the Jefferson Parish line. It has no city government of its own.

  • ParishSt. Charles
  • Population7,504 at the 2020 census
  • TypeCensus-designated place
  • ZIP Code70087

Things to Do in and Near St. Rose

  1. 01

    East Bank Levee Path

    A paved path on top of the levee, running upriver from the Jefferson Parish line to Destrehan. Good for a walk or a bike ride.

  2. 02

    Destrehan Plantation

    Next door in Destrehan. Work on the house began in 1787, and it's known as the oldest documented plantation house in the lower Mississippi Valley.

  3. 03

    Bonnet Carré Spillway

    A short drive upriver in Norco, with fishing, trails and bird watching on Army Corps land.

Landmarks The LaBranche Plantation Dependency, the Elkinsville-Freetown historical marker, River Road (LA 48)

Fun Facts About St. Rose

  1. 1In 1880, Palmer Elkins invited freed families to settle on his land here. The area became known as Elkinsville, or Freetown.
  2. 2The LaBranche Plantation Dependency, a former bachelors' quarters, joined the National Register of Historic Places in 1984.
  3. 3Beulah Levy Ledner, born in St. Rose in 1894, created the doberge cake, a layered New Orleans favorite.
  4. 4Receiver Justin Jefferson was also born here, in 1999, and played at Destrehan High and LSU before the NFL.
