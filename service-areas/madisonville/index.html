Get the House Ready

  • Lift boxes, tools and paperwork off garage and shed floors.
  • Photograph every room so there's a record of how things looked.
  • Unplug anything sitting low near an outlet.
  • Save our number where you can grab it fast: (504) 285-5666.

Stay Out of the Water

  • Floodwater can hide holes, debris and live wires, so keep clear of it.
  • Turn the power off only if the panel is somewhere dry and you can get to it without wading.
  • Children and pets belong indoors, away from the yard and garage, until it drains.
  • Ask your agent how your policy treats flood water.

Dry Out Before You Rebuild

  • Soaked carpet and pad hold water against the floor, so they come out first.
  • Drywall that sat in river water usually gets cut out above the waterline.
  • Run fans and dehumidifiers, and list what was damaged room by room.
  • When it's more than you can dry, our home water damage crew takes over.

All About Madisonville

Madisonville, on the Tchefuncte

Madisonville is a small town in St. Tammany Parish, set on the Tchefuncte River just above Lake Pontchartrain. It began as Coquille and took the name of President James Madison in the early 1800s.

  • ParishSt. Tammany
  • PopulationAbout 850 at the 2020 census
  • Named ForPresident James Madison
  • ZIP Code70447

Things to Do in Madisonville

  1. 01

    Fairview-Riverside State Park

    About 99 acres along the Tchefuncte, with campsites, a boat launch and a boardwalk through wooded wetland.

  2. 02

    Maritime Museum Louisiana

    Boat models and old photos of shipping and shipbuilding around the Lake Pontchartrain basin.

  3. 03

    The Wooden Boat Festival

    A fall weekend on the riverfront, run by the maritime museum, with wooden boats tied up along the water.

Landmarks the Tchefuncte River Lighthouse, the LA 22 swing bridge, the Water Street riverfront, the Otis House at Fairview-Riverside

River town questions

Asked in Madisonville

Will You Come Out While the River Is Still High?

Call as soon as you can. We'll time the visit for when the roads and the house can be reached without risk, and we answer 24/7.

Is Flood Water Different From a Pipe Leak?

Yes. River water brings silt and bacteria, so soaked materials usually get removed instead of only dried. Our water damage restoration page explains the process.

Can You Clean Out a Garage or Shed After High Water?

Yes. We clear out what's ruined, dry the space and sanitize the floor and walls. The sanitation page describes that last part.

What About Mold Once the Water Is Gone?

We treat it as its own job. We close the area off, cut out affected drywall or pad, and clean the framing and anything else that can stay. See residential mold removal.

Should I Call My Insurance Agent First?

Reach your agent when you can, and look over how your policy handles river flooding. You don't need to wait on that to get an estimate from us. The estimate is free.
