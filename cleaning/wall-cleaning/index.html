Painted Walls

What builds up

Smoke film, grease, scuffs and hand marks, most of all around switches and doors.

How we clean it

We start gentle, because too much scrubbing can leave a shiny patch on flat paint.

Tell us when you call

Which rooms, how high the marks go and whether the paint is flat or glossy.

Call (504) 285-5666

Brick Walls

What builds up

Soot and grime settle into the rough face and the mortar lines.

How we clean it

We work the dirt out of the texture, with cleaners chosen for the brick and the mark.

Tell us when you call

Whether the brick is painted or bare, and whether it's inside or outside.

Call (504) 285-5666

Wallpapered Walls

What builds up

Dust, smoke and stains, and water can loosen the paper at the seams.

How we clean it

We use as little water as we can and test a hidden spot first.

Tell us when you call

How old the paper is, and whether any seams are lifting.

Wall Cleaning Questions

Can Cleaning Save Me From Repainting?

Sometimes. Smoke film, grease and scuffs often come off. We look first and tell you plainly if it needs paint or repair.

Do You Clean Walls After a Fire?

Yes. Smoke film often reaches rooms the fire never did. See fire and smoke cleanup.

Do You Clean Walls in Homes and Businesses?

Both. See commercial cleaning for offices and other businesses.

What If There's Mold on the Wall?

Leave it alone until we've seen it, since scrubbing can spread it. See mold removal.
