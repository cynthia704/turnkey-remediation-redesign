Carpet Cleaning Questions

How Long Does Carpet Take to Dry?

It depends on the carpet and the air in the room. We use fans and dehumidifiers to help, and we'll tell you what to expect before we start.

Can You Get Every Stain Out?

Not every stain comes out. Some set for good. We look at it first and tell you plainly what we think we can do.

How Often Should I Have My Carpet Cleaned?

Many people do it about once a year. Pets, kids and busy rooms can mean more often. Tell us what you're seeing.

Do You Clean Carpet in Homes and Businesses?

Both. See commercial cleaning for offices and other businesses.
