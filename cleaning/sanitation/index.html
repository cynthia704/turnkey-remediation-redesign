Tile

Usually Cleaned and Sanitized

Hard, sealed surfaces don't soak things up, so they can usually be cleaned, sanitized and left in place.

It Depends on What Touched It

Soft things hold on to what spills on them. A small spill can often be cleaned. After sewage, they often can't be saved.

Usually Comes Out

Wet drywall and insulation hold on to moisture, so they usually get removed and replaced once the space is dry.

Sanitation Questions

Do You Pick Up Trash?

No. We clean and sanitize spaces. City trash, recycling and bulky item questions go to 311.

What's the Difference Between Cleaning and Sanitizing?

Cleaning takes off what you can see. Sanitizing is the step after, on surfaces that are already clean. That's why we clean first.

Can You Sanitize After a Flood or Sewage Backup?

Yes. Everything those waters touched gets treated as contaminated. See sewage backup cleanup and water damage.

How Soon Can I Use the Room Again?

That depends on what happened and how much there is to clean. We'll go over the plan with you before we start.
