Air Duct Cleaning Questions

How Often Should I Have My Air Ducts Cleaned?

There's no single schedule that fits every building. Pets, recent renovation work and heavy dust around the vents are good reasons to have them looked at. Tell us what you're seeing.

Will Duct Cleaning Help My Allergies?

We can't promise a health result, and nobody should. What we can do is clean the dust and debris out of your ducts and vents, and show you what we found.

Can You Clean Ducts After a Fire?

Yes. Smoke can travel through vents, so duct cleaning is often part of fire and smoke cleanup.

Do You Clean Ducts in Homes and Businesses?

Both. See commercial cleaning for offices and other businesses.
