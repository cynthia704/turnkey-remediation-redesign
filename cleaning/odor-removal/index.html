Smoke After a Fire

Where it hides

Walls, ceilings, carpets, curtains and the vents, even in rooms the fire never reached.

What to do now

Note where it's strongest. Leave the soot alone, since wiping smears it deeper.

What we do

We clean the soot and smoke residue that holds the smell. See fire and smoke cleanup.

A Musty or Damp Smell

Where it hides

Behind walls, under floors, in crawl spaces and anywhere water got in.

What to do now

Look for damp spots and tell us where the smell is strongest.

What we do

We look for the moisture behind it, since the smell returns if the damp stays. See mold removal.

A Sewage Smell

Where it hides

Drains, flooring and anything a backup touched, including under the house.

What to do now

Keep kids and pets away and don't try to mop it up yourself.

What we do

We clean and sanitize what it touched, then deal with the smell. See sewage backup cleanup.

Call (504) 285-5666

A Pet Smell

Where it hides

Carpets, the padding under them, furniture and the floor underneath.

What to do now

Blot fresh spots instead of rubbing, and note the places pets keep going back to.

What we do

We deep clean the carpet and furniture where it soaked in. See carpet cleaning.

Call (504) 285-5666

A Cooking or Grease Smell

Where it hides

Walls, ceilings and vents, where a thin film of grease builds up and holds the smell.

What to do now

Note which rooms it reaches and how long it's been there.

What we do

We clean the film off the surfaces holding it. See wall cleaning.

Odor Removal Questions

Can You Get the Smoke Smell Out?

We clean the soot and smoke residue that holds the smell from walls, ceilings, floors and furnishings, and treat the odor at its source.

Can You Get Rid of a Smell That Keeps Coming Back?

We look for where the smell is coming from and clean the source instead of covering it up. Tell us where it's strongest and when it started.

Will the Smell Ever Come Back?

It can, if what's causing it is still there, like a leak or damp under the floor. That's why we look for the source first and tell you what we find.
