Before you call

Cleaning Questions

Do You Clean Homes and Businesses?

Both. We clean carpets, floors, walls, ceilings and air ducts in homes and in offices and other businesses. We also handle sewage backups and odors.

What Should I Do Before You Come Out?

For a sewage backup, keep kids and pets away and don't try to mop it up yourself. For stains and spills, blot instead of rubbing. Then call us and we'll tell you the rest.

Can You Get Rid of a Smell That Keeps Coming Back?

We look for where the smell is coming from and clean the source instead of covering it up. Tell us where it's strongest and when it started.

How Do I Get an Estimate?

Call (504) 285-5666 or send the estimate form. Photos of the area help. The estimate is free.

Which Areas Do You Cover?

Greater New Orleans, the River Parishes, the Northshore, Hammond and Baton Rouge. Not sure? Call and ask.
