A Toilet Backing Up

Where it usually starts

Often a clog in that toilet's line, or further down the main line.

What to do now

Stop flushing, and keep kids and pets away.

What we do

We clean and sanitize everything it reached. A plumber may need to clear the line.

Water in the Tub or Shower

Where it usually starts

Tubs and showers sit low, so a blocked line can push water up there first.

What to do now

Stop running water anywhere in the building.

What we do

We clean and sanitize the tub, floor and anything it touched. A plumber may need to clear the line.

Call (504) 285-5666

A Floor Drain Overflowing

Where it usually starts

Floor drains are often the lowest point, so a backup can come up there first.

What to do now

Stay out of it, and stay out of standing water while the power is on.

What we do

We remove the water, then clean and sanitize the floor and walls it reached.

Call (504) 285-5666

Several Drains at Once

Where it usually starts

This usually points to the main line, since every drain in the building shares it.

What to do now

Stop using all the water, and call a plumber about the line.

What we do

We check how far it went in each room, then clean and sanitize all of it.

A Backup After Heavy Rain

Where it usually starts

Heavy rain can overwhelm the sewer system and push water back toward the building.

What to do now

Stay out of the water and keep everyone away from it.

What we do

We treat it as contaminated, so everything it touched gets cleaned and sanitized.
