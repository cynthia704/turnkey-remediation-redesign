What Does That Mark Mean?

What's on a ceiling can tell you what's going on above it. Pick the closest one.

A Brown or Yellow Ring

What it usually means

Water got in from above: a roof leak, an AC unit or a pipe.

What to do first

Stop the leak first, or the stain comes back. Stay out from under a sagging ceiling. See water damage.

What we do

Once it's dry, we clean the stain and check for damage above it.

Call (504) 285-5666

Gray or Black Film

What it usually means

Smoke rises, so ceilings often take the most, even in rooms the fire never reached.

What to do first

Leave it alone, since wiping smears it deeper. See fire and smoke cleanup.

What we do

We clean off the soot and smoke residue that holds the smell.

Dust and Cobwebs

What it usually means

Nothing urgent. Dust collects overhead, especially near vents and in corners.

What to do first

Tell us the ceiling type and height, and whether it's a home or a business.

What we do

We dust and vacuum the ceiling, choosing tools that suit its surface.

Call (504) 285-5666

A Sticky Grease Film

What it usually means

Cooking leaves a thin film that catches dust, most often in kitchens and break rooms.

What to do first

Note which rooms it reaches. Tell us if it's a home or a business kitchen.

What we do

We clean the film off with a cleaner chosen for the surface.

Call (504) 285-5666

Dark Spots That Spread

What it usually means

Spots that spread can point to moisture and mold. Humid rooms get it first.

What to do first

Leave it alone until we've seen it, since scrubbing can spread it. See mold removal.

What we do

We look for the moisture behind it and tell you what we find.
