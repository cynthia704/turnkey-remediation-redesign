Tile and Grout

What builds up

Grime builds up in the grout lines and makes the whole floor look dull.

How we clean it

We work on the grout lines as well as the tile, with cleaners chosen for the surface and the stain.

Hardwood

What builds up

Dirt and grime dull the finish, and too much water is hard on the wood.

How we clean it

We use a gentle method and keep the water to what the finish can take. Tell us how old the finish is if you know.

Luxury Vinyl Plank

What builds up

Dirt settles into the texture and along the seams between planks.

How we clean it

We use a cleaner made for the material, so the surface keeps its look. Tell us if any planks are lifting.

Vinyl and Linoleum

What builds up

It handles water well, but dirt and scuffs still build up.

How we clean it

We clean off the dirt and grime and check the seams as we go, since that's where dirt gets in.
