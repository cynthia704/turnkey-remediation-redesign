Before you call

Fire and Smoke Questions

Can You Get the Smoke Smell Out?

We clean the soot and smoke residue that holds the smell from walls, ceilings, floors and furnishings, and treat the odor at its source.

When Can We Go Back Inside?

Only after the fire department says the building is safe. Then call us, and we'll go through what can be cleaned and what needs repair.

What About the Water From the Fire Hoses?

We handle water damage too, so drying out the water from putting the fire out is part of the same job.

Do You Work on Homes and Businesses?

Both. Kitchen fires at home, and fire and smoke damage in offices and other businesses.

How Do I Get an Estimate?
