Right after a fire

What to Do First After a Fire

Four things that keep you safe and make the cleanup easier.

Call (504) 285-5666
  1. 1

    Wait for the All Clear

    Don't go back inside until the fire department says the building is safe.

  2. 2

    Take Photos First

    Photograph and video the damage before anything gets moved or thrown out. It helps if you file a claim.

  3. 3

    Leave the Soot Alone

    Wiping soot smears it deeper into walls and fabric. Let us clean it the right way.

  4. 4

    Don't Mix Cleaners

    Bleach and ammonia together make toxic fumes. If you clean anything, use one product at a time.

What fire leaves behind

It's More Than What Burned

Most of the cleanup is in the rooms the flames never reached.

Call (504) 285-5666

Soot and Smoke Residue

Sticky and acidic, it settles on walls, ceilings and furniture and keeps damaging surfaces the longer it sits.

Smoke Odor

Smoke works into walls, carpets and furniture. The smell stays until the residue holding it is cleaned.

Water From Putting It Out

Fire hoses soak floors and walls. That water needs drying out too.

Tarnished Metal

Smoke is acidic, so silver, brass and other metals can tarnish quickly.
