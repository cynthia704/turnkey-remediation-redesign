Where Mold Likes to Hide

New Orleans is warm and humid, and mold needs moisture. Tap a part of the house to see why it collects there.

Call (504) 285-5666

Attic

Poor airflow keeps moisture trapped up here, and a roof leak adds more.

Look for: dark patches and a musty smell.

Bathroom

Bathrooms stay damp, and without good airflow the moisture has nowhere to go.

Look for: dark patches creeping across the ceiling.

Kitchen

Kitchens are often damp, and a pipe defect can keep feeding the problem.

Look for: discoloration on the walls and near pipes.

Outside Walls

Outside walls can get cold and sweaty, and that's enough for mold to get started.

Look for: patches that range from black to green or white.

Crawl Space

Poor airflow keeps moisture trapped under the house, where you rarely look.

Look for: a musty smell indoors, even with no mold in sight.

If you've spotted it

What to Do and What to Leave Alone

Do This

  • Stop the leak if you can do it safely.
  • Keep the room closed off.
  • Note where you see it and when you first smelled it.
  • Call us, or send photos with the estimate form.

Leave This Alone

  • Don't scrub or disturb it. That can send spores into clean rooms.
  • Don't ignore a musty smell because you can't see anything. It can be behind a wall or under a floor.
  • Don't wait on a leak. Mold needs moisture to keep growing.

Keeping It Dry Keeps It Away

Mold needs moisture. These three habits lower the chance of it coming back.

Fix Leaks Fast

Control the Humidity

A dehumidifier helps in a city this humid.

Keep the Air Moving

Before you call

Mold Questions

Should I Clean It Myself?

We suggest leaving it alone until we've seen it. Scrubbing can spread spores, which is why we contain the area before removing anything.

Could a Musty Smell Mean Hidden Mold?

It can be. A musty smell can point to growth hidden behind a wall or under a floor. Call us and tell us where it's strongest.

Why Does Mold Keep Growing Here?

New Orleans is warm and humid, and mold needs moisture. A leak, poor airflow or high humidity gives it what it needs.

Do You Work on Homes and Businesses?

Both. Bathrooms, kitchens and crawl spaces at home, and offices and other commercial buildings.
