A Musty Smell With Nothing to See

What it can mean

Mold can sit behind a wall or under a floor. If it's a smell without the moisture, see odor removal.

What to do now

Note the rooms where it's strongest and when you first smelled it.

Dark Patches on a Ceiling or Wall

What it can mean

Patches from black to green or white usually have moisture feeding them.

What to do now

Leave them alone and keep the room closed off.

Tell us when you call

Which room, how big the patches are and whether the spot feels damp.

Spots Around a Window

What it can mean

Outside walls can get cold and sweaty, and that's enough for mold to get started.

What to do now

Dry up any standing water and leave the spots alone.

Tell us when you call

Which side of the house, and whether the window leaks when it rains.

Mold After a Leak

What it can mean

A leak keeps feeding it until the water stops. See water damage if it's still wet.

What to do now

Stop the leak if you can do it safely, and take photos.

Tell us when you call

Bathroom

Kitchen

Attic and Crawl Space

Take a look after a big storm, since you rarely see these spots.

Whole House

Before you call

Home Mold Questions

Should I Clean It Myself?

We suggest leaving it alone until we've seen it. Scrubbing can spread spores, which is why we contain the area before removing anything.

Do I Have to Leave the House?

It depends on where it is and how much of the house is affected. We'll go over the plan with you before any work starts.

Will It Come Back?

Mold needs moisture. Fixing the source and keeping things dry lowers the chance of it coming back.

Does Homeowners Insurance Cover It?

That depends on your policy and what caused the moisture. Your agent can tell you what applies.
