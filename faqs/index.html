Both. Water, fire and smoke, sewage and odor problems in homes, and cleanup and deep cleaning for offices and businesses.

What Should I Do Before You Get Here?

Stay safe first. If you can do it safely, stop the water at the main shutoff, keep people and pets away from sewage, and don't go back into a fire-damaged building until it's cleared. Then call us and we'll walk you through the rest.

Water Damage

What Should I Do First If I Have Water Damage?

If you can do it safely, shut off the water at the main. Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Then call us.

How Long Does Drying Take?

It depends on how much got wet. Drying can take several days, and we check that everything is dry before repairs finish.

Do You Handle Flood and Sewage Water?

Yes. Floodwater and sewage are treated as contaminated, so everything they touched gets cleaned and sanitized.

Fire and Smoke

What Happens During Fire and Smoke Restoration?

We go through what can be cleaned and what needs repair. Then we clean the soot and smoke residue, treat the odor at its source and dry out any water from the fire hoses.

When Can We Go Back Inside?

Only after the fire department says the building is safe. Then call us, and we'll go through what can be cleaned and what needs repair.

Can You Get the Smoke Smell Out?

We clean the soot and smoke residue that holds the smell from walls, ceilings, floors and furnishings, and treat the odor at its source.

Mold

How Can I Tell If I Have Mold?

It can show up as fuzzy or spotted patches on walls, ceilings or furniture. It can also hide behind a wall or under a floor, and a musty smell can point to it. Call us and tell us where it's strongest.

Should I Clean It Myself?

We suggest leaving it alone until we've seen it. Scrubbing can spread spores, which is why we contain the area before removing anything.

Why Does Mold Keep Growing Here?

New Orleans is warm and humid, and mold needs moisture. A leak, poor airflow or high humidity gives it what it needs.

Cleaning and Odor

What Cleaning Do You Offer for Businesses?

Carpet, floor, wall, ceiling and air duct cleaning, for offices and other businesses. We also handle sewage backups and odors.

How Often Should I Have My Air Ducts Cleaned?

There's no single schedule that fits every building. Pets, recent renovation work and heavy dust around the vents are good reasons to have them looked at. Tell us what you're seeing.

Can You Get Rid of a Smell That Keeps Coming Back?

We look for where the smell is coming from and clean the source instead of covering it up. Tell us where it's strongest and when it started.
