Follow the water

Which Floor Did It Start On?

In a commercial building, water doesn't stay where it started. Pick a floor to see where it goes.

Call (504) 285-5666

The Roof

Where it usually starts

A roof leak, a clogged roof drain or a leaking rooftop unit.

Where it goes

It comes through the top floor ceiling, often before anyone sees it on the roof.

What to do first

Move people and equipment out from under it, then call us.

Call (504) 285-5666

An Upper Floor

Where it usually starts

A burst pipe, a restroom overflow or a broken supply line.

Where it goes

Down. Through the ceiling and into the floors below, including tenants who don't know yet.

What to do first

Shut off the water if you can do it safely, and warn the floors below.

Call (504) 285-5666

A Middle Floor

Where it usually starts

A pipe in the wall, a break room appliance or a restroom.

Where it goes

Across the floor, into the walls and down through the ceiling below.

What to do first

Shut off the water if you can do it safely, and move records and equipment up off the floor.

Call (504) 285-5666

The Ground Floor

Where it usually starts

Heavy rain or flood water coming in at street level.

Where it goes

Into flooring, the bottom of the walls and anything stored low.

What to do first

Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Floodwater counts as contaminated.

Call (504) 285-5666

The work

Five Steps in the Building

Call (504) 285-5666

Step 1

Check How Far It Went

We check every affected area, including hidden spots, and measure moisture.

Step 2

Pump Out the Water

Pumps and vacuums pull out the standing water.

Step 3

Dry It Out

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry floors, walls and materials. This can take several days.

Step 4

Repair the Damage

Damaged drywall, insulation and other materials get replaced.

Step 5

Final Check

We check that all areas are dry and look for anything missed.

Commercial Water Damage Questions

What Should We Do First?

If you can do it safely, shut off the water at the main. Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Then call us.

How Long Does Drying Take?

It depends on how much got wet. Drying can take several days, and we check that everything is dry before repairs finish.

Do You Handle Flood and Sewage Water?

Yes. Floodwater and sewage are treated as contaminated, so everything they touched gets cleaned and sanitized. See sewage backup cleanup.

Can You Clean the Building Afterward?

Yes. See commercial cleaning for floors, carpets, walls, ceilings and air ducts.
