Bathroom

What usually goes wrong

A toilet or tub overflow, or a leak inside the wall.

What to do first

Shut off the valve behind the toilet or the water at the main. If it's a sewage backup, keep kids and pets out.

What we check

The floor, the wall behind it and any room that shares that wall.

A washing machine hose or a leaking water heater.

What to do first

Shut off the water at the main. Stay out of standing water while the power is on.

Flooring, nearby walls and any room that shares them.

Living Room

What usually goes wrong

Storm or flood water coming in low, or water that spread from another room.

What to do first

Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Floodwater counts as contaminated, so keep kids and pets away.

What we check

Flooring, the lower walls and the insulation behind them.

Attic and Ceiling

What usually goes wrong

A roof leak or storm damage. A stain or a sagging ceiling often shows up before any dripping.

What to do first

Stay out from under a sagging ceiling. Move what you can out of the way, then call us.

What we check

Insulation, drywall and how far the water traveled.

Crawl Space

What usually goes wrong

On a raised home, water can sit under the floor where you can't see it. A musty smell or a soft floor can be the first sign.

What to do first

Stay out if there's standing water down there. Call and tell us what you're noticing.

What we check

Insulation, the floor above it and moisture levels.

At your house

What Happens Once We're There

Five jobs, in this order.

Call (504) 285-5666

  1. Find How Far It Went

    We check how far the water went, including hidden spots, and measure moisture before we start.

  2. Pump Out the Water

    Pumps and vacuums pull out the standing water.

  3. Dry It Out

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the floors, walls and everything the water reached. Depending on the damage, drying can take several days.

  4. Clean and Sanitize

    The affected areas are cleaned and sanitized to deal with contaminants and odors.

  5. Repair and Final Check

    Damaged drywall, insulation and flooring get replaced, and we check that everything is dry.

Home Water Damage Questions

What Should I Do First?

If you can do it safely, shut off the water at the main. Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Then call us.

Can You Save My Furniture and Belongings?

Often some of it can be cleaned and dried. Tell us what got wet and we'll go through what can be saved and what can't.

How Long Does Drying Take?

It depends on how much got wet. Drying can take several days, and we check that everything is dry before repairs finish.

Do You Handle Flood and Sewage Water?

Yes. Floodwater and sewage are treated as contaminated, so everything they touched gets cleaned and sanitized.

Do You Work on Businesses Too?

Yes. See commercial water damage restoration.
