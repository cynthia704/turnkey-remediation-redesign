Know what you're dealing with

Not All Water Is the Same

Where the water came from decides how it gets cleaned up.

Call (504) 285-5666

Clean Water

From sources without harmful contaminants.

Examples: Broken supply lines, overflowing sinks, rainwater coming in.

Grey Water

Carries some contaminants but isn't highly toxic.

Examples: Washing machines, dishwashers, toilet overflows without waste.

Black Water

The most contaminated kind.

Examples: Sewage backups and floodwater.

Hidden Water

The water you can't see: behind walls, under floors and inside ceilings, often from slow leaks.

How we find it: moisture readings, including the hidden spots.

Before you call

Water Damage Questions

What Should I Do First?

If you can do it safely, shut off the water at the main. Stay out of standing water while the power is on. Then call us.

How Long Does Drying Take?

It depends on how much got wet. Drying can take several days, and we check that everything is dry before repairs finish.

Do You Handle Flood and Sewage Water?

Yes. Floodwater and sewage are treated as contaminated, so everything they touched gets cleaned and sanitized.

Do You Work on Homes and Businesses?

Both. Burst pipes and roof leaks at home, and flooding and soaked walls in offices and other businesses.
